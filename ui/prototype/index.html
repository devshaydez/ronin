It protects itself.

If something looks wrong, Ronin stops first and explains after. When things settle, it restarts on its own.

PauseDeposits stop when rates or data look unsafe. Withdrawals and payouts keep working.

ExitIf a pool fails its checks, funds are pulled out and held safe in the vault.

Self-healOnce rates return to normal, the vault resumes with no one pressing a button.

Built on Reactive Network.

Event driven

A change on chain wakes the contract. Nothing polls on a timer.

No keeper

Across chains

Verifiable

What Ronin does not do.

Testnet onlyNo real money. Mock pools are marked as demo.
No bridgingFunds stay on their chain. Only rates and safety signals cross.
AI is a gateIt suggests. The vault checks the rules again before moving anything.
